Our Story

A cup you'd swear
you've had before.

Déjà Bru started as a question: what if a coffee shop felt less like discovery, and more like return? Every detail — the low amber light, the hiss of the machine, the first sip — is built to feel already familiar, even on your first visit.

We roast in small batches, pull each shot to order, and treat the ritual of coffee-making the way it deserves: unhurried, deliberate, a little bit sacred.
