Our Story

Heritage & Warmth in the Heart of Gwalior

Nestled in the cultural heart of Gwalior — a city that gave the world tansen's ragas and the skyline of Madhya Pradesh — Hotel Centella is a sanctuary where heritage architecture meets quiet modern comfort.

From hand-carved furniture sourced from local craftsmen to farm-fresh regional cuisine, every detail is shaped by a single intention: to let you feel the soul of this royal city from the moment you arrive.
