Our Story

A dream of everything vegetarian, under one roof

The founders of Param Food Complex are vegan by choice — and they always dreamt of a place where vegetarian food isn't the side of the menu, but the whole menu. Not one cuisine, but many: the chaat corner, the farsan counter, the sizzler plate, the dosa griddle and the bakery case, all living together like stalls in one busy, happy bazaar.

That dream became a Gwalior institution on Moti Mahal Road, Padav — a family place where breakfast idlis roll into lunchtime thalis, evening pakoras and late-night sizzlers, without the kitchen ever taking a break.

Everything is made in-house with fresh, earth-friendly ingredients: healthy sandwiches, creamy spaghetti, wholesome soups and salads, and a huge family of desserts and drinks.
