Our story

Slow food, in a fast world.

Hearth & Crumb began in a home kitchen with a jar of wild starter and one stubborn belief: good bread cannot be rushed. Twelve years on, we still mix, shape and score every loaf by hand.

“We bake the way our grandmothers did — with time as the main ingredient.”

Our flour is stone-milled from organic grain grown within a hundred miles. Our butter is cultured. Our ovens are lit before sunrise, so everything on the shelf was baked the same morning you buy it.
