Our Philosophy

Less noise.
More cup.

We believe the best coffee doesn't announce itself. It arrives quietly, does exactly what it promises, and leaves you wanting the next one. Deja Bru works directly with smallholder farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — selecting lots that reward attention.

"Every roast is a decision about what to leave alone."

Our roastery operates a few blocks from the cafe. Beans move from green to bag in under 48 hours. We don't chase trends. We chase traceability.
